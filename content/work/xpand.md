Our research began with a focus on less than social people. From early interviews comfort zones seemed to be a hot topic among people with difficulty socializing. We found that shared common experiences and confidence in one’s own knowledge can help people overcome social fears. Which is why our team created a knowledge and skill sharing product. Follow our journey.

***Word Frequency Study** Word frequency was established. These word were compared between three categories that were extracted from the question *(How often do you step out of your comfort zone?)* The categories are: Regularly, Sometimes, Never. The word were then compared with all other responses. We found that one participant from the smallest word pool category (NEVER) had the most unique vocabulary. It as if she spoke a different langue than all other participants.*

***Idea generation** Through some ideation and story-boarding our team came up with our final design. It is a place for people to learn and exchange knowledge through the hands on experience of completing a desired project. Xpand as it came to be known allows people to market what they know so that other people may learn from their expertise.*

![](/work/xpand/020.png)

![](/work/xpand/021.png)

***Stakeholder circle** At the center is the user. The circles closest to the user are the stakeholders most accessible to the user. Xpand blends the outer most circle with the ease of entry similar to Thingyverce, Lynda and Youtube.*

![](/work/xpand/022.png)

***Experience Journey** We found that users became intrigued at the idea of becoming a full time instructor of what they currently do as a passion in their spare time. This pursuit would create a tightly formed group of student and hobbyist withing the particular mastery. This community could bring many positive outcomes to the local city (pop up shops, new markets, more income, tax revenue).*
