## Suggested title & subtitle
**Title:** Bita
**Subtitle:** A preventative dental health ecosystem that connects patients, dentists, and insurance providers through a smart brushing kit, a companion app, and shared habit-tracking — turning routine oral care into a collaborative, incentivized relationship between all three stakeholders.

## Research & Discovery

### Patient Interviews — Key Findings (two rounds, slides 002 & 007)
![Slide 2](/work/bita/002.JPG)
![Slide 7](/work/bita/007.JPG)

**Round 1 — Likes:** Simple/easy scheduling-cancelling systems; Comfortable offices; Innovative dental experiences; Transparency on procedures/costs/times; Friendly and empathetic doctors; Minimum appointments and fees; Strong relationships.
**Round 1 — Motivations/Goals:** We go when we have problems; Our job requires us to go; We don't see the importance; Go for regular check-ups; Make sure there's no trouble; Free cleanings because of insurance.
**Round 1 — Others:** We pick doctors by recommendations/reviews; All dentist offices have specific smells; We always have to call to book or cancel; We prefer SMS reminders; We usually go once or twice a year.

**Round 2 — Pain Points:** Painful and non-consented procedures; Impatient doctors; Insurance and financials are unclear; Appointment fees are discouraging; Long treatments and processes; Lack of trust in doctor's advice; Endless waiting; Feeling ignored and unvalued; Insurances don't cover all treatments.
**Round 2 — Reasons for No-Shows:** Out of our control situations; Just forgot about it; Insurance issues; Work-personal issues; Trust issues; Feeling unattended or ignored.
**Round 2 — Expectations:** Knowledgeable practices; Clean and able to treat problem; Quick and efficient procedures; Clear and transparent fees; Coordination with Insurance.

### Doctor Interviews — Key Findings (slides 003 & 006)
![Slide 3](/work/bita/003.JPG)
![Slide 6](/work/bita/006.JPG)
**Pain Points:** Patients not being able to communicate for no shows; Third parties for bookings conflict with our schedules; Hard to predict how much time each patient might take; Small clinic hence can't order materials in bulk.
**Reasons for No-Shows:** Do not measure no-shows; Staff unsure if no-shows are because of their own practice; Doctors believe no-shows could be personal reasons or costs.
**Expectations:** Patients can come directly to the office without referral; Patients need to be better consumers.
One interviewee quote: "I don't overbook. That comes with his vision of practice." / "My office is all about educating the patients and work on an appointment only schedule."

### Survey Data (slide 004)
![Slide 4](/work/bita/004.JPG)
60% have missed an appointment; 71% have dental insurance; 47% find cancellation fees acceptable; 94% currently cancel by phone; 58% prefer SMS; 48% average satisfaction with booking availability.
Max wait time willing to tolerate for walk-in: 30 min 35%, 1 hour 35%, 2 hours 12%, Indifferent 18%.
Reasons for missing appointments: Not Relevant 36%, Time Conflict 29%, Cost/Insurance 18%, Transportation 16%.

## Defining the Solution

### Value Proposition Canvas (slide 013)
![Slide 13](/work/bita/013.JPG)
**Business side — Products/Services:** "Bita App - Tools for oral Health awareness - Data receiver and processor - Feedback - Advice - Communicative Platform"; "Bita Brush Kit - Brush - Swab - Floss - Data collector."
**Gain Creators:** Connect with expert for real-time consultation/advice; Social Awareness; Oral health awareness; Time management; Simplify healthcare information; Tracking progress; Dental insurance transparency.
**Pain Relief:** Receive expert guidance; Listen and learn from others' roadmap; trusted relationship; Booking with purpose; Oral health/insurance status knowledge; treatment management tools.
**Customer side — Gains:** Make right decision; knowledge; Proactive; Awareness; Self-care interest; Mindful; Engaging in oral health; Follow recommendations; Participate in sessions; Attend and engage in dentist appt.
**Pains:** Lack of support; Unstable finance; High cost assistance services; Lack of communication; Stress; Helpless; Lack of control.
