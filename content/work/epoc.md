In a world where information doubles every year, one cannot take full advantage of the resources available. E-consciousness helps the user filter out irrelevant data to bring precise useful information. Epoc AI manages all data inputs so the user won't have to, giving the user close to real-time actionable knowledge. The Epoc family of products aims to manage a user's IoT devices and mental trends, to make the right useful information available to the user.
