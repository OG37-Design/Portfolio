## Section 0 — Overview / Executive Summary (slides 001–004)
Four-slide front-loaded summary (concept → problem → approach → outcome), each tagged bottom-left: "Topic of Interest," [Problem], "Approach," "Expected outcome."

### Topic of Interest — E Consciousness
> **E Consciousness:** The external awareness of AI as an extension of a person's being.
>
> In our current technological environment we are very dependent and occupied with our dexteral and visual modality.
>
> Even with this concentrated attention we are still unaware of much of the information available.

Visual (001): Grayscale illustration of a person's head "opened" like a lid, spheres/bubbles rising out — external AI awareness extending consciousness.

### UX Problems (to be solved)
> Lack of accurate and clear Information/Data provided to the user in real time by prioritizing on Trust and Privacy factors.

Visual (002): Horizontal priority ranking: Trust, Information Fatigue, Privacy, Syntax, Health.

### Approach — Information Environment
> **Context** Providing more opportunities and Information for an user to act on the Every day life around them.
>
> **Content** Personalized Information and Variedly depending on a multitude of factors unique to the user (by machine learning technique)
>
> **User** Multitasking and always looking for more and accurate Information.

Visual (003): Three-circle Venn diagram — Context / Content / User.

### Primary Research — Surveys and Data
> Questionnaire is based on users thought and preemptive action conducted prior and during the drive home from work. The Questionnaire is structured so as to get Information about the users wants, activities, schedule, spontaneous thinking and decision making prior, and after leaving work to home.

Focal survey question: *"What are you thinking about during the drive from work back to your home?"* Other questions: What do you like to do on your drive home from work? / What stops do you make between work and the drive back home? / What do you usually plan to do before getting home? / What information do you wish to have before getting home? / When do you decide what to do after leaving work? / How often do you make spontaneous decisions after leaving work? / When do you find yourself being most productive at work?

Visual (006): raw verbatim survey responses table — "What I'm going to do when I get home" / "Food mostly" / "Random.stuff. things to do" / "What to cook or eat" / "Life" / "Work, food, Netflix" / "What I have to do when I get home." / "What was left over from work or personal stuff waiting at home" / "Getting rest" / "What I'm going to eat when I get home" / "Rest" / "tasks I need to get done before going to bed" / "what a wastw of life and efforts. i should do something of myself." / "What's for dinner.." / "Probably about how my day was" / "Anything that's important" / "How the day was." / "Random thoughts" / "Pick up kids and think about days work" / "What to eat for dinner" / "Usually listening to music or talking and relaxing my mind." / "What to do at home"

### User Journey Map (slide 008)
> From the survey results we sought to map a users journey. The journey begins 60 minutes prior to the end of the workday and ends in bed time. Through this exercise the team found several areas of opportunity/pain points, these are marked in red.

Timeline: red dashed = "Current Journey," red boxes = pain points. Stage 1 (60/50/30/15 min till work ends) → Stage 2 (work ends → first stop, +20 min) → Stage 3 (home +45 min → bed time). Pain points:
- "54% of users plan what they will do after leaving at this time range" (60 min)
- "90% of people are least productive during this time" (50 min)
- "The stress from last minute work and the stress from the plannign after work task mounts" (30 min)
- "Information is limited as to what is waiting for us at home, making it hard to plan" (15 min)
- "User tries to gather information that he was not able to find during work" (work ends, and again at first stop)
- "User arrives at the grocery store and begins to walk the isles in search of what they need to make their preferred dish"
- "User looks for the recipe he had in mind, the user find he missed an item. But he will go through the recipe." (Home)
- "Leisure time is far shorter with out epoc" (Bed time)
- "User enjoys the meal and washes [watches] a TV show"

## Section 3 — Naming: "Huic" → "Epoc" (slides 011–019)
Same concept developed/tested under working name **Huic** before renaming to **Epoc**. Research questions, market map, and journey map reused nearly identically under both names.

**HUIC** (hu:'i:k) — "an encouraging call given by a huntsman during a hunt to cheer on hounds."

Storyboard (013, grocery store): Bambino: "Hey! Huic. Can you help me buy Some fruits and toast bread?" Huic: "Hey! Bambino. Welcome back! Yeah, sure. I can help you with it. Please follow me." / "How was your day bambino?"

Huic journey map (014): same pain-point timeline, relabeled "HuicJourney." Zoom detail: "The customer is greeted at the entrance by Huic. Huic and the user agree to a recipe." → "Huic guides the customer in search of his items" → "User pulls up the recipe Huic agreed to and begins to make the dish."

Rebrand to **Epoc** (blue), same concept-footing content.

Storyboard (016, shopping): man sees shoe priced "$300," scans at register ("$$$$"): "Hey! epoc look for the lowest price" → "Epoc has results that include coupons to arrive at the lowest price" → phone screen with BUY button.

Shopping IA flowchart (017): Voice command / Photo inputs → Input decision → Decoder → Cloud service → Cloud computes → Action Update → "Has action terminated?" (No loops / Yes → end). Parallel: Cloud computes → Hit sent → Notification setting → Phone Mode (On/Off/Silent/Sleep/Airplane) → Notification sent (vibrate) → User acknowledges → Accept/Decline (No loops / Yes → Action initiated).

Storyboard (018, drive home): device gives breakdown (Time left at work, Drive time to home, Fridge options, Gas options). User: "Hey! Epoc. Thanks for the Info but I want to eat out today." Device broadcasts to friends: "Happy hour at Vault meet see you there!" Epoc: "Hey! Bambino The Vault has happy hour until 8 pm today." John (FB): "Vault hell yeah see you there." Sara (IG): "Let's get our drink on."

Comparative journey chart (019): red dashed "user guide" (unassisted) dips at pain points vs. green "Huic" and blue "epoc" lines staying smoother/elevated.
