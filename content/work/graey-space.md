## Solution / Product Design (slides 008–012)

### Flat Pack Furniture (slide 008)
![Slide 8](/work/graey-space/008.png)
> "Both options Folding Post/Sweeping Post are designed to fit in the same dimension packaging. Allowing for the shipment of 36 per standard US Pallet."

Diagram: exploded flat-pack packaging — box outline, flattened/uncoiled felt strip, pole with notched joinery and hardware, nested into an isometric shipping carton with barcode — shows DTC packaging/logistics thinking (pallet shipping efficiency).

### Folding Post (slide 009)
![Slide 9](/work/graey-space/009.png)
> "A solution for the casual every Items and Graey Space clothe" *(sic — likely meant "casual everyday items")*

A felt object made of multiple open-ended cone/petal shapes on a wooden cross-foot base, pink background, pale pink clothing tucked into the petals, canvas sneakers nearby for scale. The shorter, multi-cone variant for frequently-worn "everyday" items.

### Sweeping Post (slide 010)
![Slide 10](/work/graey-space/010.png)
> "A solution for the casual weekly Items and Graey Space clothe" *(sic — same grammatical issue)*

The taller, single continuous coil variant: full-height coiled felt column, ball caps on top peg, shirts draped over the coils, standing on a skateboard deck against a blue background — a more casual/streetwear styling. Positioned as the solution for items re-worn across "the casual weekly" cycle.
